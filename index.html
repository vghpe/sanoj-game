Sanoj - A Music Trivia Game for Friends
HOW TO PLAY
  • Drag a track onto its year.Tap a track, then a year. Only a skill can it, and only locked tracks clear the decade.
  • Badges narrow it down. Most skills leave a BADGE on the track, and they stack.
  • The answer is the album year, not the single.
  • Spend hearts on skills. Start with 7 — reach zero and you lose.
