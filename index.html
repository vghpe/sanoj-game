HOW TO PLAY
  • Drag the tracks out of the Crate onto the years you think they belong to.
  • The answer is the year the track’s album came out — not the single, not the chart date. Where the two disagree, the album wins.
  • You’ll clear 1 decade at a time.
  • Coins are your lives. You start with 7, they carry from decade to decade and never refill and are spent on skill cards to help place tracks. Run out and the run ends there — how far back you got is the result.
